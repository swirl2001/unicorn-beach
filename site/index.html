What's a unicorn day?

Three or more daylight hours in a row with:

≤ 1 ftwaves just off China Beach
≤ 5.6 mphwind
≤ 9 mphgusts

From 2021 to 2025 this happened about twice a year, mostly October to January, usually in the morning when a small, long-period swell meets a calm high-pressure day.

Waves: CDIP, Scripps Institution of Oceanography, and Open-Meteo for days 4–7. Wind, rain and advisories: National Weather Service. Chances are estimates from forecasts, not guarantees. Always check conditions, currents and shorebreak on the beach yourself.
